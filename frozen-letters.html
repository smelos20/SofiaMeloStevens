Frozen Letters

A solo narrative environment built in Unreal Engine 5, telling the story of a daughter waiting for letters from her father that stopped coming.

RoleEnvironment Artist
TeamSolo project
FocusLighting & storytelling
Timeline10 weeks · Personal project

My role

Concept, environment art, and narrative direction. I built the space, hand-painted the textures, and shot camera angles alongside development to plan the final trailer.

  • Designed and modeled the full interior and exterior environment
  • Hand-painted textures in Procreate over UV bases for a stylized look
  • Directed camera angles and shot progress footage for the trailer
  • Built the environment around a single emotional story

Story

A father who worked far away from home still remained present in his daughter's life by exchanging letters with her. They wrote to each other regularly to stay connected, since the place where he worked had very poor signal. The letters, which used to arrive every week, suddenly stopped coming. His daughter grew worried and tried to call him, but her calls went straight to voicemail. Concerned, she left a message...

Process

From there I refined the layout and started populating the space. As I finished the interior, I kept adding a TV, a mirror, and shelves. Near the end, I shifted focus to the exterior rocks, a road, and more buildings to make the surroundings feel as lived in as the interior.
