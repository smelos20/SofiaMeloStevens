Project Lead: Raccoon Rampage

A Global Game Jam project where I led a team of 18 people to create a game in 48 hours: a bullet-heaven style game where players defeat enemies to unlock new rooms and upgrade their weapons, racing to take them all down.

EngineUnreal Engine 5
Team18 junior designers
TypeGlobal Game Jam
Timeline48 hours
Download playable game

My role: Producer / Project Lead

I led a team of 18 junior designers to build a bullet-heaven style game in 48 hours for Global Game Jam, built around the theme "Mask." With only two team members handling technical implementation and the rest working as designers, my primary responsibility was scope management, balancing ambition against what was realistically achievable while still ensuring the team could ship a complete, playable game.

  • Led and managed a team of 18
  • Served as the bridge between tech and art, ensuring implementation was correct so systems functioned as intended
  • Directed the environment design to stay cohesive with the "Mask" theme and support the bullet-heaven gameplay loop
  • Modeled environment assets and implemented items into the world
  • Helped create the trailer

Forming the team

My friends and I wanted to participate in the Savannah Global Game Jam. We started recruiting, and slowly people joined until we had a team of 18. I took the role of producer and led the team, and together we created Raccoon Rampage in 48 hours.

Concepting and production

For the first hour, we concepted ideas and created a basic story so we could design characters and the environment. Everything had to stay in scope given our limited time. Our game used procedurally generated rooms, so we only had to model around the basic room measurements.

Producing under pressure

As producer, I set specific timelines for the team to hit so we could finish everything within 48 hours. Managing the team in person was easy, but when snow hit Savannah we couldn't stay together and migrated to Discord to finish the game remotely.

Environments

Since we had a big team of artists, I was able to model a few things, then move straight into implementing them in the game. With the procedural rooms, I created 10 different room designs from the models these artists made.

Trailer

After the game was done we also created a trailer. I used all the assets we had to build a cinematic sequence in Unreal Engine that tells a story. I really liked telling a story with the assets and camera.

Takeaways

I learned a lot about the video game pipeline and how to balance art and tech to reach a result in a very short time. Leading a team was a great experience, and I hope to work with them again.
